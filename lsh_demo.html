In the real system, movie vectors live in 384 dimensions — impossible to draw directly. The box above is a 3D stand-in: the green plane is one hyperplane slicing the space in two. Every vector gets bit = 1 if it lies on the same side as the normal arrow, bit = 0 otherwise. With num_bits planes you divide space into 2num_bits buckets. A single plane may accidentally split two similar movies; using num_tables independent tables (each with freshly drawn random planes) ensures similar movies share a bucket in at least one table.
